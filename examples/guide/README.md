# Guide examples

Every `.hrs` here was transpiled, compiled and run. Numbered files are the programs the language guide's snippets were taken from; `30_tutorial.hrs` is the tutorial's finished program.

The `l*.hrs` files were the demonstrations of four limits that have since been fixed. `l1.hrs` and `l3.hrs` now run; `l2b.hrs` and `l4.hrs` are now rejected by Harsh itself with a message naming the fix, rather than producing Rust that fails to compile. `p2.hrs` and `p4.hrs` are the isolated-closure forms that replaced them. `spec1.hrs` and `spec2.hrs` are the two semicolon examples that serve as the specification.

`do1.hrs` shows `do:` as a block opener on every construct; `w1.hrs` shows the three `where` forms.
