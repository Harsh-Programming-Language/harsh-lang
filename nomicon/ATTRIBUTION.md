# Attribution

*The Harshonomicon* is a Harsh companion to **The Rustonomicon**
(https://doc.rust-lang.org/nomicon/, source https://github.com/rust-lang/nomicon),
by the Rust Project developers, licensed under the Apache License 2.0.

This companion follows that book's structure and choice of topics, chapter by
chapter, with prose of its own -- shorter than the original, on purpose -- and
every program written in Harsh, built and run. Each page links to the original
section, the place for the full explanation. The Harsh edition is licensed
under the Mozilla Public License 2.0, as Harsh is.
